struct CGAL::Homological_discrete_vector_field::Surface_mesh_io< TriangleMesh, Traits >::iterator< HDVFType >

Iterator over homology/cohomology generators of an Hdvf computed over a complex built from the initial surface mesh.

Given a perfect HDVF, iterates over critical cells and associated homology/cohomlogy generators encoded as Simplex_chain.
