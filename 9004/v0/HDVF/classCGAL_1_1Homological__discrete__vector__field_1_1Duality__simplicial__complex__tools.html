template<typename CoefficientRing, typename Traits, typename SparseMatrixStruct = CGAL::OSM::Sub_sparse_matrix_structure<CGAL::OSM::Sparse_chain>>
class CGAL::Homological_discrete_vector_field::Duality_simplicial_complex_tools< CoefficientRing, Traits, SparseMatrixStruct >

The class Duality_simplicial_complex_tools is dedicated to Alexander duality for 3D surface meshes.

Starting from a simplicial chain complex (encoding a 3D surface mesh), it provides methods to embed the complex into a larger icosphere and generate a 3D constrained Delaunay triangulation.

Technically, starting from a Simplicial_chain_complex K_init, the methods dualize_complex() builds a Simplicial_chain_complex L and a Sub_chain_complex_mask K.

  • L : complex built out of K_init together with a closing icosphere
  • K (Sub_chain_complex_mask) : Sub_chain_complex_mask identifying K_init inside L

Two dualize_complex() methods are provided, based repsectively on tetgen and CGAL::Conforming_constrained_Delaunay_triangulation_3.

Generates a subcomplex \(K\)K and a complex \(L\) with \(K\subseteq L\) from a set of simplices mesh_io (based on tetgen).

On the one hand, a Simplicial_chain_complex is built from mesh_io (let us call if _K); on the other hand, this complex is embedded into a larger icosphere and a 3D constrained Delaunay tetraedrization is generated. Then \(K\), \(L\) and vertex coordinates are extracted and stored in a Complex_duality_data structure.

After the function, mesh_object_io has been extended with the simplices of the closing icosphere.
