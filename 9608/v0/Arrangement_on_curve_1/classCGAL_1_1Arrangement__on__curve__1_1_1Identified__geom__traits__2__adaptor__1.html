class CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Identified_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1< GeomTraits_2 >

Identified_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1 adapts a 2D geometry traits class of the 2D Arrangements package, whose parameter space has identified left and right sides, into a 1D geometry traits class whose parameter space is identified.

The points of the 1D arrangement are points of the 2D traits that lie on a single x-monotone master curve whose two ends lie on the 2D identification curve: its minimal end on the left boundary and its maximal end on the right boundary of the 2D parameter space. Both ends are the same point of the surface, namely the identification point of the 1D arrangement. A point is the identification point if and only if it lies on the 2D identification curve. The remaining points are ordered lexicographically, by x and then by y, which is the order along the master curve. Pair this traits with Identified_topology_traits.

An example of a suitable 2D traits is Arr_geodesic_arc_on_sphere_traits_2, where the 2D identification curve is the meridian that separates the left and right sides of the parameter space.
