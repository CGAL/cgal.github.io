class CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Right_closed_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1< GeomTraits_2 >

Right_closed_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1 adapts a 2D geometry traits class of the 2D Arrangements package into a 1D geometry traits class whose parameter space is open on the left side and closed on the right side.

The points of the 1D arrangement are points of the 2D traits that lie on a single master curve, which is either an x-monotone curve or a vertical segment. The right boundary point, provided at construction, is the lexicographically largest point of the master curve; the left endpoint of the master curve is not a point of the 1D arrangement. Points are ordered lexicographically, by x and then by y. Pair this traits with Right_closed_topology_traits, which Default_topology_traits selects for it.
