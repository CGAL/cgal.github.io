Definition

template<typename DecoratedTraits_2>
class CGAL::Arr_tracing_traits_2< DecoratedTraits_2 >

A metadata traits-class decorator for the arrangement package.

It traces the invocations of traits-class functors. It is parameterized with another traits class. For each traits method it prints out its input parameters and its output result

It models all the concepts that the original traits models.

Examples
Arrangement_on_surface_2/count_and_trace.cpp.
