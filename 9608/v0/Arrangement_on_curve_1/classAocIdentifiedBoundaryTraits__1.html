AocIdentifiedBoundaryTraits_1 Concept Reference
Reference Manual » Concepts » Geometry Traits Concepts

Definition

Models of the concept AocIdentifiedBoundaryTraits_1 handle curves on a circle or a curve that is topological equivalent to a circle. The circle is oriented in such a way that the boundary of the parameter space, the circle is the mapping of which, is identified on the left and right sides.

Refines
AocTraits_1

When both categories are CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Identified_side_tag, the traits must also provide the functor type Is_on_x_identification_1 (and its accessor), which tests whether a point lies on the identification point.
