template<typename GeomTraits_2>
class CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Closed_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1< GeomTraits_2 >

Closed_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1 adapts a 2D geometry traits class of the 2D Arrangements package into a 1D geometry traits class whose parameter space is closed on both sides.

The points of the 1D arrangement are points of the 2D traits that lie on a single master curve, which is either an x-monotone curve or a vertical segment. The left and right boundary points, provided at construction, are the lexicographically smallest and largest points of the master curve, respectively. Points are ordered lexicographically, by x and then by y. Pair this traits with Closed_topology_traits.
