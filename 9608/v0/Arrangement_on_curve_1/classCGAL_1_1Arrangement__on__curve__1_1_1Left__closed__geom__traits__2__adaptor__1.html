template<typename GeomTraits_2>
class CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Left_closed_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1< GeomTraits_2 >

Left_closed_geom_traits_2_adaptor_1 adapts a 2D geometry traits class of the 2D Arrangements package into a 1D geometry traits class whose parameter space is closed on the left side and open on the right side.

The points of the 1D arrangement are points of the 2D traits that lie on a single master curve, which is either an x-monotone curve or a vertical segment. The left boundary point, provided at construction, is the lexicographically smallest point of the master curve; the right endpoint of the master curve is not a point of the 1D arrangement. Points are ordered lexicographically, by x and then by y. Pair this traits with Left_closed_topology_traits, which Default_topology_traits selects for it.
