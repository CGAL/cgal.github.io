Definition

template<typename Kernel>
class CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Line_3_traits_1< Kernel >

Line_3_traits_1 is a geometric traits model of AocTraits_1 designed for subdivisions along an infinite supporting line embedded in 3D Euclidean space.

Points are represented using standard 3D Cartesian coordinates, and their relative sequence is computed by mapping positions across the directional trajectory vector of the 3D line.
