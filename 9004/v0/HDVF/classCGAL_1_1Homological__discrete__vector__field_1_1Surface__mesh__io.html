template<typename TriangleMesh, typename Traits>
class CGAL::Homological_discrete_vector_field::Surface_mesh_io< TriangleMesh, Traits >

The class Surface_mesh_io is an intermediate IO class, used to load the collection of simplices of a triangle mesh.

These collections are then used as input to produce Abstract_simplicial_chain_complex.

Type encoding chains on a surface mesh.

A chain over a surface mesh is a formal linear combination \(\lambda_1\sigma_1+\cdots+\lambda_k\sigma_k\) of cells \(\sigma_i\) of the surface mesh (all of the same dimension), with coefficients \(\lambda_i\) in CoefficientRing (a model of EuclideanRingWithoutDivision).

A Simplex_chain encodes such a chain as a map between halfedges (each cell \(\sigma_i\) is identified by its associated halfedge) and coefficients ( \(\lambda_i\))

template<typename TriangleMesh, typename Traits>
CGAL::Homological_discrete_vector_field::Surface_mesh_io< TriangleMesh, Traits >::Surface_mesh_io ( const TriangleMesh & mesh)

Constructor from a triangular mesh.

Loads vertices, edges and triangles of a surface mesh mesh (called the underlying triangle mesh). Vertices and simplices are identified by their index together with their dimension. In a given a dimension, indices coincide with the enumeration order of corresponding surface mesh iterators.

As vertices are identified by their index, vertices with similar underlying points and different vertex indices will be considered as different.
