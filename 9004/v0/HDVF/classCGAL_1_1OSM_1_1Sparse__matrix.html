template<typename CoefficientRing, int StorageFormat, template< typename, int > typename SparseChainType>
class CGAL::OSM::Sparse_matrix< CoefficientRing, StorageFormat, SparseChainType >

The class Sparse_matrix implements the concept SparseMatrix, that is, sparse matrices optimized for topological computations.

It provides standard linear algebra operators and fast iterators and block operations (set, get and nullify) which are required to implement efficiently HDVFs.

Given a SparseChain model, users should normally define a Sparse_matrix_structure structure (which provides both corresponding Sparse_chain_type and Spare_matrix_type templates).

The implementation is based on mapped sparse matrices. Hence matrices of the Sparse_matrix class are either column of row major (the StorageFormat parameter determines this storage format). A column-major (resp. row-major) Sparse_matrix is a vector of SparseChainType (a model of SparseChain) which encode columns (res. rows). Moreover, in order to efficiently iterate over non empty columns (resp. rows) the Bitboard data structure implements the concept SparseMatrix::NonZeroChainIndices. A bitboard is basically a bucket of bits recording the indices of non empty chains. However, this data structure has been designed in order to efficiently remove or add indices, as well as provide efficient iterators to visit non empty chains.

For instance, let us consider the \(5\times 4\) matrix:

\[A = \left(\begin{array}{cccc} 1 & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ -1 & \cdot & 2 & \cdot\\ \cdot & \cdot & 1 & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \end{array}\right) \]

where \(\cdot\) means \(0\).
