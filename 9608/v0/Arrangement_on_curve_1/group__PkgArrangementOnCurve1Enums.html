enum class CGAL::Arrangement_on_curve_1::Interval_parameter_space
strong

#include <CGAL/Arrangement_on_curve_1/Parameter_space.h>

The scoped enumeration Interval_parameter_space indicates where the pre-image \(t\) of a point lies in the interval parameter space \(X\) of the master curve.

It is the return type of the functor AocTraits::ParameterSpaceInX_1, which every model of the concept AocClosedBoundaryTraits_1 provides.
